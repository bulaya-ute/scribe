    How it works
    
            
    The browser decodes the file's audio track locally, resamples it to 16 kHz,
            and runs Whisper (base, quantised) through WebAssembly. The markdown is assembled in
            the page — there is no backend processing step to send anything to.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    What to expect
    
            
    Transcription runs faster than real time on a laptop, slower on a phone.
            Very long videos (over ~45 minutes) can run out of tab memory — split them first.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Accuracy
    
            
    A small on-device model: clear speech transcribes well, heavy accents and music
            less so. Names and numbers are the usual weak spots — skim before publishing.
